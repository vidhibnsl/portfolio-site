Seismic Forest

Worldbuilding · Cinematic Environment

Seismic Forest world built for the short film 'Tell Him I Left', which is still in production. The seismic forest represents a sanctuary at the border of two countries that have been at war for the last millenia. People that live here have learnt to adapt to its tumultuous veins and find a certain sense of peace away from the wrath of human extremism.
